Family movie night is a tradition worth protecting. In a sea of options, finding shows that are genuinely uplifting — funny without being crude, exciting without being fearful — can feel like a treasure hunt. The good news? Wonderful, wholesome options are out there.

For younger children, *Bluey* has won hearts worldwide with its gentle humor and its beautiful picture of a loving, playful family. *The Chosen* offers older kids and parents a moving, faith-centered look at familiar Gospel stories. Classic series like *Little House on the Prairie* and *The Waltons* still shine with warmth, hard work, and family loyalty.

For a cozy night in, consider films like *The Princess Bride* for a mix of adventure and laughter, or animated favorites like *Ratatouille* and *Up* that carry big-hearted lessons about courage and love.

Make the night a full experience. Pop some popcorn, dim the lights, and let the kids build a blanket fort. Afterward, talk about the story together: *What did the character do that was kind? What would you have done?* These conversations turn screen time into heart time.

A simple tip: keep a shared "family watchlist" so everyone gets a turn choosing. It teaches patience and gives quieter kids a voice.

Screens aren't the enemy when they draw us together and point us toward what's good, true, and beautiful. Chosen well, a family show can spark laughter, gratitude, and conversations that last long after the credits roll.
