Once a week, try switching everything off — and switching your family on. A screen-free night can feel awkward for the first ten minutes and magical for the next two hours. Here are ten simple ideas to get you started.

1. **Board game tournament.** Rotate classics like Uno, checkers, and Candy Land. Keep a family scoreboard on the fridge.
2. **Build a fort.** Blankets, chairs, and couch cushions become a castle. Read stories inside by flashlight.
3. **Bake something together.** Cookies, bread, or a simple pizza. Little hands love to knead.
4. **Backyard star-gazing.** Lay out a blanket and find shapes in the sky. Talk about how big and beautiful creation is.
5. **Family talent show.** Everyone performs — even the parents. Laughter guaranteed.
6. **Storytelling circle.** One person starts a story; each person adds a sentence.
7. **Nature scavenger hunt.** A quick list — a smooth rock, a red leaf, a bird's song.
8. **Puzzle night.** Work a jigsaw together with cocoa.
9. **Dance party.** Play joyful music and let loose in the living room.
10. **Kindness cards.** Make notes for neighbors, teachers, or grandparents.

The point isn't to fill every minute — it's to be present with one another. Some of the best moments will come in the pauses: a silly joke, a deep question from a child, a hug that lingers.

Protecting time like this tells your family that people matter more than pixels. Try one night this week. You may find it becomes everyone's favorite.
