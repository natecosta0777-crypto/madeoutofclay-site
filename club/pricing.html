Frequently Asked Questions

Can I support the library today?
Supporter checkout is not currently open. You can email us for an update, and free library access does not depend on payment.
Do I need a credit card to join?
No. Free membership requires no credit card or payment information.
Can I print the books?
Use the printables offered with the library for personal or classroom activity use. Book pages remain subject to the site's copyright terms unless a download is specifically provided.
What ages are these books for?
Our picture books are designed for children ages 3–8 (preschool through 2nd grade), though many older children and even adults find them meaningful too. The stories, language, and themes are intentionally accessible across a range of developmental stages.
Are the books neurodiversity-affirming?
Yes — that's central to our mission. Every Made Out of Clay book is written and illustrated with neurodivergent readers in mind. We use identity-first language, celebrate sensory differences, feature Autistic and ADHD characters, and avoid ableist tropes. We never use puzzle-piece imagery.
Do you offer classroom or school pricing?
We do! We offer special plans for educators. Visit our Schools & Libraries page for details on classroom and school-wide subscriptions with discussion guides, lesson plans, and bulk pricing.
Does supporting unlock extra books?
No. The current reading library is available to free members. Any future supporter option will remain separate from reading access.
How do I access the read-aloud audio?
Narrated read-alongs are still being produced and will appear on titles as they are ready. We do not promise audio on every current title yet.
