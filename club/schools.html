Books That Belong in Every Classroom

A picture-book library designed to affirm neurodivergent readers. Selected stories use identity-first, deficit-free language and celebrate sensory and communication differences without cure narratives.

About the perspective: these resources are produced by a family-run studio and informed by lived family experience. They are reading resources, not clinical guidance or a substitute for a school's qualified educators and specialists.
