Good to know

Questions, answered

Is it really free?

Yes. The current digital library and printable activities are free. No account, email, card, or payment is required to read.

What ages is it for?

The digital Book Club is generally best for ages 3–9. The separate print books on the main site are labeled for ages 2–7.

What makes this different from Epic! or Amazon Kids+?

We're not trying to be a warehouse of 40,000 books. We're a small, curated shelf with a point of view — affirming, social-emotional and brave-girl stories — each with a printable activity. Quality and values over volume.

What about the read-alongs? Where's the audio?

Narrated read-alongs are coming soon — they're being recorded and edited now, and good audio takes real work to get right. Every digital book and printable is available today, and narration will roll out to titles as it's ready.

Do I have to share my child's name?

No. First-name personalization is optional, is presented to an adult, and stays only in that browser on that device. Feeling check-ins are not saved or transmitted.
