Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Bottom line up front: We collect a parent’s email so we can send the book club. We don’t track your child, we don’t sell anyone’s data, and you can leave anytime with one click.

Who this is for

The Made Out of Clay Digital Book Club is designed for children but signed up for and managed by a parent or caregiver. Accounts and emails belong to the grown-up, not the child.

What we collect

  • Your email address — so we can send the weekly letter, printables, and club updates.
  • A child’s first name, only if you choose to enter it — used to personalize the clubhouse greeting. It lives in your own browser (local storage) and is not required.
  • Basic visit data, only after an adult accepts — Google Analytics and Metricool help us understand which pages are used. Optional analytics do not load before consent. Ad personalization and Google Signals are disabled, and IP anonymization is requested where supported.
  • Membership and inquiry details — Web3Forms processes parent or educator form submissions so we can receive them. Our newsletter subscription worker processes the email used to join the weekly letter list.
  • Technical delivery data — Cloudflare processes request and device information needed to host, secure, and deliver this site.
  • Browser storage — this site uses local storage for the adult's privacy choice, membership access on that device, and optional first-name personalization.

What we don’t do

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your email or any data.
  • We do not run third-party behavioral advertising inside the club.
  • We do not require a child’s name, birthday, photo, or location.

How we use your email

  • To send the weekly club letter, new printables, and occasional updates.
  • To answer you if you write to us.
  • Every email includes a one-click unsubscribe. Leaving removes you from future sends.

Children’s privacy (COPPA)

We aim to comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Because sign-up is by a parent or caregiver and we don’t collect personal details from children, the club is built to keep kids’ data out of the picture. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, email us and we will delete it promptly.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe anytime using the link in any email.
  • Ask us to delete your email and any associated data.
  • Clear the child’s name we stored in your browser by clearing your site data.
  • Use the “Privacy choices” button in the footer to accept or decline optional analytics.

Questions or requests

Email clay@madeoutofclayprod.com for anything privacy-related — access, deletion, or just a question. A real person will answer.
