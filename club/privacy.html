Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Bottom line up front: Reading does not require an email. We collect an adult’s email only when that adult requests parent notes or contacts us. We do not load optional Google Analytics in the child experience, save feeling check-ins, or sell personal information.

Who this is for

The Made Out of Clay Book Club is designed for children to use with a parent, caregiver, educator, or other responsible adult. There is no reading account. Email subscriptions, inquiries, privacy choices, and optional first-name personalization are for adults to manage.

What we collect

  • Your email address — when an adult chooses to receive occasional parent notes, new-story announcements, printables, or club updates. Email is not required to read.
  • A child’s first name, only if you choose to enter it — used to personalize the clubhouse greeting. It lives in your own browser (local storage) and is not required.
  • Optional visit data, only after an adult accepts — Google Analytics helps us understand which adult-facing marketing pages are used. It does not load in the Clubhouse, Bookshelf, Reader, Characters, or Mailbox. Ad personalization and Google Signals are disabled.
  • Hosting and performance data — Cloudflare processes request and device information needed to deliver and secure the site and may run its edge performance beacon on public pages. This is separate from optional Google Analytics and is covered by the site’s security policy.
  • Inquiry and subscription details — Web3Forms processes parent or educator form submissions so we can receive them. Our newsletter subscription worker processes an adult's optional email signup.
  • Technical delivery data — Cloudflare processes request and device information needed to host, secure, and deliver this site.
  • Browser storage — this site uses local storage for an adult's privacy choice, the last-opened book, and optional adult-managed first-name personalization.
  • Feeling check-ins — the Clubhouse response changes only the words shown on the current page. The selected feeling is not saved, transmitted, or added to analytics.

What we don’t do

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your email or any data.
  • We do not run third-party behavioral advertising inside the club.
  • We do not require a child’s name, birthday, photo, or location.

How we use your email

  • To send optional parent notes, new printables, and occasional updates requested by an adult.
  • To answer you if you write to us.
  • Every email includes a one-click unsubscribe. Leaving removes you from future sends.

Children’s privacy (COPPA)

We designed the Book Club to minimize collection from children: reading needs no account, optional Google Analytics does not load in child-experience routes, feelings are not saved, and first-name personalization stays on the device under an adult gate. Cloudflare still processes the limited request and performance data described above to host and secure those routes. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it promptly. Our operational choices and this notice should be reviewed as the service or its third-party tools change.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe anytime using the link in any email.
  • Ask us to delete your email and any associated data.
  • Clear the child’s name we stored in your browser by clearing your site data.
  • Use the “Privacy choices” button in the footer to accept or decline optional analytics.

Questions or requests

Email clay@madeoutofclayprod.com for anything privacy-related — access, deletion, or just a question. A real person will answer.
